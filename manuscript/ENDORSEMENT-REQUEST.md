# arXiv endorsement — how to ask

arXiv emails you a six-character endorsement code when you start a cs.LG submission. Any active author
in any cs.* category can endorse. Find candidates on the abstract page of a recent paper you cite
("Which authors of this paper are endorsers?"). Write to ONE person at a time; arXiv considers mass
requests inappropriate. Good first candidates are authors whose work the report confirms or builds on:
the authors of "Accuracy is Not All You Need" (Dutta et al., NeurIPS 2024), RULER (Hsieh et al.),
HELMET (Yen et al.), KIVI / KVQuant, DFlash, and EvalPlus. A USP faculty member who publishes in cs.* is
also a legitimate and often faster route.

## Message (adapt the first line to the person)

Subject: arXiv endorsement request (cs.LG) — measurement report that independently confirms your <paper>

Dear Dr. <name>,

I am an independent researcher and a professional master's candidate in Data Science at the
Universidade de São Paulo. I have written a 39-page technical report measuring quantization, long
context and speculative decoding for Qwen3.8-27B on two 16 GB consumer GPUs. One of its results is an
independent confirmation of <their finding, one clause> on a different model family and hardware
class, and it cites your work in Section 2.

As a first-time arXiv author I need an endorsement for cs.LG. If you are willing, my endorsement code
is <CODE> and the link is https://arxiv.org/auth/endorse?x=<CODE>. The draft and all data are public:
https://github.com/henrique-simoes/llm-quantization-damage (PDF at manuscript/tex/main.pdf).

I understand if you would rather not; endorsement only states that the work is appropriate for the
category, not that you agree with it. Thank you for your time either way.

Luiz Henrique Simões
