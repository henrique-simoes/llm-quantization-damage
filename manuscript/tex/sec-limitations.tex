\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{One model, one machine, one engine family.} Every number is for Qwen3.8-27B in Unsloth
Dynamic GGUF builds on two RTX~5060~Ti cards with llama.cpp builds from August--September 2026. The
hybrid attention layout (KV in 16 of 64 layers) makes the context arithmetic unusually favourable;
dense models of this size will not reach these windows on this memory. The split optima, ceilings and
speeds are specific to this host. One operator, mostly one seed.

\paragraph{There is no full-precision reference.} BF16 weights do not fit, so every divergence is a
distance from a 6-bit build along a ladder. The reference's own damage is unmeasured and is zero here
by construction. Unsloth's published figures suggest it is small \citep{unsloth_dynamic3}; I did not
verify that. The reference build \QsixXL{} is also not the same file as the \Qsix{} arm carried into
the second battery.

\paragraph{Divergence is measured at 2K tokens, on prompt tokens, on two corpora.} Not at depth, not on
generated text, not on other programming languages, natural languages or tool-calling formats. The
calibration data of these builds is not published in detail, so a bias toward Wikipedia-like text
cannot be excluded. The tool's standard errors assume independent tokens (\cref{sec:multiplicity}).

\paragraph{Long-context task accuracy is thinly measured.} RULER's retrieval question at 131K on the
multi-key task is open (\cref{sec:ruler}); the fix (a generous output budget, reasoning off) was
identified and not run. AA-LCR covers about 100K tokens, not 262K, with $n=100$ and a full pass for one
drafter only. No code-editing task was evaluated at depth for any build; every agentic run used
prompts near 40K tokens. Nothing in this report licenses ``accuracy holds at 250K''.

\paragraph{The cache precision is validated only at short context.} The \flag{q4\_0} cost (KLD 0.003)
was measured at 2K tokens on one build and one corpus. Outside evidence suggests far larger effects at
depth (\cref{sec:kvq}). In the second battery, build and cache precision change together, so their
effects cannot be separated.

\paragraph{Task benchmarks are underpowered by construction.} That is a finding, and also a limit: a
null here means ``not separated at this $n$'', never ``equal''. HumanEval+ bounds the 4-bit to 6-bit
difference at about 3 points; AA-LCR and GPQA bound it at roughly 10. A difference of one or two points
could exist and would be invisible to everything I ran except divergence.

\paragraph{Sampling.} Historical rows used greedy decoding, which matches neither of Qwen's presets, so
their absolute values are not comparable to any published score. The model card's own benchmarks
(LiveCodeBench v6, SWE-bench Pro, Terminal-Bench) were not run. The non-equivalence result for
speculative decoding is greedy-only, and its mechanism is not established.

\paragraph{Speed and energy.} Speed is single-stream, single-slot. Batch serving was out of scope. The
first battery's speed cells ran at temperature 0.7 and carry text-driven variance; the second
battery's are greedy with $k=8$ per cell, which is small. There is no wall-power sensor; system power
is modelled; no controlled energy-per-token curve at depth exists. Thermal asymmetry was observed, not
controlled.

\paragraph{Cancelled and incomplete work.} A breadth wave (speculation at temperature above zero, the
presence-penalty setting, the draft cache precision) and the energy curve were cancelled to stay
within budget. The vLLM context battery was stopped before its deep-prefill arm, so its ceiling is
boot-verified only; the stable-versus-nightly discrepancy (98K against 51K) is unresolved. SGLang was
attempted with a speculative worker only. Several context failures at the default split are single
attempts. Six intermediate GGUF builds were never tested.

\paragraph{Historical rows cannot be re-measured.} The engine image behind everything before
2026-08-29 is gone, with two model files. Those rows are labelled and kept apart. The evidence chain
for most numbers ends in server logs that are too large to publish; the repository carries the derived
artifacts and verbatim extracts. By the usual badge definitions this is ``artifacts available'', not
``results reproduced''.

\paragraph{Judged benchmarks depend on the judge.} AA-LCR totals moved across a 5-item band among
three judges, and a handful of questions with arguable keys decide every small-sample contrast.

\paragraph{Process.} AI agents executed the campaign and assisted the analysis and the writing, under
my direction. That speeds up work and errors alike; \cref{sec:corrections} is the record of how many of
the latter were caught, and cannot say how many were not.
