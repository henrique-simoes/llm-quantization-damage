# Methodology-diagram style guide — multivac / *Divergence Ranks What Benchmarks Bound*

Applies to the three schematics: F20 (the correction record), F21 (the measurement design) and
F25 (the defect taxonomy).

## Form

Clean academic schematic. Boxes with 1-pt borders and generous internal padding; thin connectors;
sans-serif labels; white background. No drop shadows, no gradients, no 3-D, no clip-art or icons
beyond a single optional anchor glyph marking the reference arm in F21. Arrowheads are small and
solid.

## Colour

At most two accent colours per diagram — `#0072B2` for the divergence path and `#D55E00` for the task
path — plus greys. The diagram must remain legible in greyscale, so any grouping carried by colour is
also carried by position, border weight or an explicit label.

## Rules that are not negotiable

1. **Every literal in the source text traces to a figure or table in the paper.** Do not add, round or
   alter a number, a date or a note identifier. A diagram that returns with a value not present in its
   source text is a planner hallucination and must be discarded, not corrected in an image editor.
2. **Do not summarise away the uncomfortable content.** F20 lists claims this project published and
   later changed; F25 lists its own instrumentation defects. Both are offered as method. Softening
   them defeats the figure.
3. Use the caption supplied in the manifest verbatim.
4. Keep every column, lane and band that the source text specifies, in the order it specifies.
