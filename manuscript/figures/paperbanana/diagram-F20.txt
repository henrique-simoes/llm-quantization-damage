THE ONE SENTENCE THIS FIGURE MUST COMMUNICATE
The study corrected itself sixteen times in five days, fourteen of them at zero GPU cost, because
per-item records were retained — and one of the corrections corrected a correction.

STYLE
Clean academic timeline. Thin rules, sans-serif labels, white background, no drop shadows or
gradients. Two accent colours at most (#0072B2 and #D55E00) plus greys; lanes distinguished by
position and label, not by colour alone.

CONSTRAINTS
This is offered as method, not confession — but it must not soften the facts. Every node names a
real claim that was published inside this project and later changed. Do not aggregate the nodes into
a reassuring summary such as "the study was mostly right": the count is the finding. Do not invent
dates, note ids or claims.
